About

Having trained in the UK and internationally, I hold a Master’s degree in Performing Arts from Rose Bruford Drama School. My work spans text-based and physical theatre, with additional experience in singing.

Alongside stage training, I have worked on a range of film sets, developing a sensitivity to the subtle, internal language of screen performance.

I am drawn to work that resonates across cultures — performances that access something instinctive and shared. I am particularly interested in projects that explore emotional depth, stillness, and transformation.
